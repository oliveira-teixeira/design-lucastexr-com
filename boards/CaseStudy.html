Overview

Fetchly is a product team for hire: engineering, design, QA and a dedicated project manager on one monthly plan. The website has to make that offer clear to a founder who is comparing agencies. I directed the V2 program from March to August 2025, from competitor benchmarking and a brand kit through the sitemap, wireframes, page designs and the Webflow build.

Since October 2025, as Director of Design, I own the site. I design the pages in Figma and build the harder sections myself as React code components for Webflow.

Challenge

The old site sold services as a list. It said nothing about how the work actually runs, and the pages that mattered most for new business depended on paid traffic to be seen at all.
