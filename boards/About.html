Process

How I work

  1. 01

    Discovery

    Every project starts with a two week discovery sprint. I interview stakeholders, audit what already exists and log every gap in a register, so we agree on the problem before anyone draws a screen.

  2. 02

    Domain and flows

    I model the domain in UML: who the users are, what data moves and which systems talk to each other. User and data flows come before prototyping, which keeps design and engineering working from the same map.

  3. 03

    Prototype and test

    I build prototypes people can actually use and instrument them with Microsoft Clarity or Hotjar. Watching real behavior settles design debates faster than any meeting.
